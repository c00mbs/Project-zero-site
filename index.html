THE PLANET
ISN'T EMPTY.

Earth, 2990. The last traces of civilization are buried beneath forests, rust and silence.

You awaken in a forgotten underground laboratory. Your identity is redacted. Your designation is Subject Zero. Outside, a planetary intelligence watches over a world it believes it saved.

But when an impossible signal calls your name, the system that controls Earth begins to ask questions it cannot answer.
